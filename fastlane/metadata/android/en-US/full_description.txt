HitOrMiss is an offline reaction and decision game with 40 comic portraits.

Tap the picture when the adult is visibly armed. If the person is harmless,
let the countdown expire without tapping. Every correct decision earns one
point. Tapping a harmless person or missing an armed person ends the round.

Three difficulty levels start at 5, 3 or 1 second per picture. Every three
correct decisions reduce the time by 10%, down to a minimum of 0.3 seconds.
Each difficulty has its own local top-three leaderboard.

The game works entirely offline and has no ads, tracking, purchases, account
or network permission. The interface is currently in German.

All 40 comic images were generated for this project with AI. Only adults
appear armed. Children only appear on harmless cards. No blood or injury
is depicted. The game includes visible guns and combat knives.

Leaving the app aborts the active round without a leaderboard entry.
