The original HitOrMiss artwork is dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal,
to the extent that any copyright or related rights in that artwork exist
and are held by the project's rights holder. The complete legal text is in
`LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`.

This applies to:

- All 40 `app/src/main/assets/images/*.jpg` game cards.
- The original icon at `app/src/main/res/drawable/icon.xml` and the store PNG icons.
- `safe-overview.jpg` and `armed-overview.jpg`.

The game cards were generated for this project using OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool. `ARTWORK.md` records the prompts. All depicted people are
fictional. Children only appear on harmless cards. No claim of exclusive
copyright in AI-generated output is made. AI-generated origin is disclosed;
it does not imply approval of the app by OpenAI or F-Droid.

The original program code, documentation and build scripts are MIT licensed.

license and copyright notices. The Apache license is supplied in
`LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`.

The Android system typeface is used at runtime; no third-party font is bundled.

Maintainer-provided real-device screenshots are distributed as project
documentation under the MIT license. Embedded original game artwork retains
its CC0 dedication. Screenshots are genuine captures, not generated artwork.
